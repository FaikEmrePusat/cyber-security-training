## What “spotlight” means (your question on rule 7)

**Short answer:** In *your* revised plan, spotlight is **not** “ignore four domains all week.”

| Term | Meaning for you |
|------|------------------|
| **Weekday queue** | Every weekday Usta walks **five domain blocks** (cyber, language, faith, books, music), one after another. Each block is capped at about **45–50 minutes**. |
| **Today’s main focus (spotlight)** | If the day is short or something slips, **career blocks first**: cyber (Ledger / homework) and German after Oak. Faith, books, and music still belong on the same day, but they are next in the queue—not “deleted.” |
| **Weekend deep focus** | Saturday/Sunday **10:00–14:00** (no Oak): personal **software or cyber projects**. Also: **batch** Medium/LinkedIn drafts for the *next* week. Weekdays you mostly **publish/share** what is already ready. |

So: spotlight = **priority when time is scarce** + **weekend project/content deep work** — not “only touch two hobbies per week.”

## Locked weekday rules

### 1. Oak (you said OK)

- **When:** Weekdays **10:00–14:00**, timezone **Europe/Istanbul**.
- **Rule:** During Oak, Usta’s only command is school/class presence (capture one line max if needed). No music, no long Ledger session, no books as the main order.

### 2. Five daily domains (your revision — replaces the old tiny-only floor)

After Oak (and the short transition), every weekday you still do **all five** areas in small pieces. That is intentional: five short blocks, not five endless projects.

| Order | Domain | What “done” means for that day (minimum) |
|-------|--------|------------------------------------------|
| A | **Cyber** | Real work in Cyber Ledger and/or Oak homework / your own cyber study — one serious block, ≤45–50 min unless you choose to continue after the queue. |
| B | **Language (German)** | Follow the existing German plan for that day — one block ≤45–50 min. |
| C | **Faith** | At least: **one page** Qur’an + its meaning/tafsīr; **one bab** Cevşen + meaning; **one page** from your other religious sources. (Usta can split these into sequential Next Actions so you never see three faith tasks at once.) |
| D | **Books** | About **10 pages** of your current book. |
| E | **Music** | Open ney **or** piano and try a piece. Daily theory study is **not** required. |

**Attention rule (you said):** If the unit of work stays around **45–50 minutes**, focus holds. Usta must therefore **cut blocks at ~45–50 min** and say “Done for this block — next domain,” instead of open-ended sessions.

**Journal:** Not listed in your five domains. Treat as **optional** evening lines unless you ask to put it back in the required queue.

### 3. Medium / LinkedIn (your revision)

- **Weekend:** Prepare / batch what you will publish the **following** week.
- **Weekday:** Prefer **only share/publish** what is already prepared — do not invent a full drafting session on a weekday unless the five domains are already cleared.

### 4. Energy / focus (your revision)

- You said focus does not drop when **the next action is clear** and the block is ≤45–50 min.
- Keep an optional **“Energy low”** escape for rare bad days (floor = cyber + German only, or stop after faith). It is a safety valve, not the default lifestyle rule.

### 5–6. Ledger boundary + content (you said OK)

- Ledger = cyber + German craft only.
- Faith/book prompts use **your** pasted templates and sources — no bundled scripture in the app.

### 7. How we measure a good day

- Did the **five weekday domain blocks** get a real pass (or an honest carry with a reason)?
- Did **weekend** project time and/or content batch happen when planned?
- We do **not** score “I touched eleven random apps.”

### 8. On-ramp + building (your revision)

- Use the app **while** we improve it.
- Do **not** freeze all development for 14 days.
- Do **not** either: spend those days only redesigning instead of pressing Done. Parallel means: **daily queue first**, feature work in bounded sessions.
